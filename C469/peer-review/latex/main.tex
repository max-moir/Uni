This paper outlines a proposal for the development of a robot to assist in the caretaking of elderly and disabled people.

It is stated that the main goal of the paper is ``to demonstrate the capabilities of a mobile personal assistance robot using `simple’ image processing.''

It cites two papers that detail applications similar to the intended goal.


\section{Decision}
The final decision of this review is that this paper requires significanct revision.
Although the topic is well motivated, the execution falls short in several regards, as outlined below.
